Signal rules

BUY: lower wick > upper wick, then upper > lower, then lower > upper. Trigger: live price crosses above candle 3 high. Stop reference: candle 1 low.

SELL: upper wick > lower wick, then lower > upper, then upper > lower. Trigger: live price crosses below candle 3 low. Stop reference: candle 1 high.

Experimental scanner. Candles are built from ticks collected after scanning starts. Higher timeframes need time to build. Tick-built candles may differ from MT5 candles. Keep Chrome open and verify every setup on MT5 demo before considering live trading.

No orders are placed. No Deriv password or API token is requested. Reloading the page clears collected data.
